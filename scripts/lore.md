# LIV Dynasty — condensed canon for the LIV Network newsroom

Current canon date: 2026 offseason, post-rookie draft, before the 2026 regular season.

## The universe
LIV Dynasty is a 12-team dynasty fantasy football league presented as a corporate universe.
Each owner is CEO of a permanent corporation listed on the LIV Dynasty Exchange. Trades are
mergers and acquisitions, picks are R&D capital, players are assets, championships are earnings.

League format that shapes takes: superflex (QB depth is king), TE premium, 0.5 PPR for RB/WR,
14-week season with two matchups scored per week (hence records like 23-5), 7 playoff teams,
only the 1 seed gets a bye. 24-man rosters plus taxi squads mean waivers are usually stale —
the trade market is where the action is. The 2027 rookie class is viewed as unusually strong,
so 2027 first-round picks carry a premium.
